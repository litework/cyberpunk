## Use it

**https://litework.github.io/cyberpunk/** — nothing to install, works on phones, and works offline. On a phone, *Add to Home Screen* installs it as an app with its own icon. Your progress stays in your browser; SHARE carries it to another device.

## What it does

<table>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/fcee0a/fcee0a.png" alt=""> <b>Mission tree</b></td><td>The full flowchart, redrawn as vectors so it stays sharp at any zoom. Select a tile and the card opens in the left panel: who gives the mission, where, what it needs, and what it unlocks — as rows you can tick or open, so a requirement chain can be walked without leaving the chart. Every card links to the mission on the Cyberpunk Wiki.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/4fe0e6/4fe0e6.png" alt=""> <b>Table sheet</b></td><td>The same missions as a sortable table — <b>TABLE</b> beside <b>TREE</b>, or press <code>T</code>. Main missions sit under Prologue, Acts 1–3, Epilogue and Phantom Liberty (acts as we place them; the chart has none), every other category under its own heading. Same search, filters, ticks and NEXT as the tree. An act rail shows done / total per act, and each act header can tick every story mission before it in one go.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/4fe0e6/4fe0e6.png" alt=""> <b>Requirement lines</b></td><td>Dashed lines run from the selected tile to what it needs (teal) and what it unlocks (yellow), so a requirement is a place on the chart, not just a name.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/4fe0e6/4fe0e6.png" alt=""> <b>Search and filters</b></td><td>Plain text, or type a fixer or district and press <b>Tab</b> to filter by it. Categories filter from the grid or the KEY.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/ff69c8/ff69c8.png" alt=""> <b>Progress</b></td><td>Tick the box on any list row, on any tile, or on the card — or double-click a tile. Mark a mission <b>Missed</b> when it has closed for good, and NEXT stops pointing at it. <b>Hide done</b> takes finished rows out of the list. Kept in your browser in up to three named playthroughs; <b>SHARE</b> copies a link that carries the active one, <b>Export</b> saves them all as a small file and <b>Import</b> reads it back. Each category shows done / total.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/fcee0a/fcee0a.png" alt=""> <b>Save image</b></td><td>Downloads the current view as a PNG at twice the screen's resolution — chart, backdrop and your marks, no UI.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/fcee0a/fcee0a.png" alt=""> <b>Next up</b></td><td><b>NEXT</b> jumps to the next main mission you haven't done and nothing on the chart locks — Phantom Liberty before Act 3 once you've begun it, side content only once the story is caught up. Rows show <b>READY</b> or <b>LOCKED</b> from the chart's requirement notes and your done marks — nothing on the chart says you can't, not a guarantee.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/ff2d55/ff2d55.png" alt=""> <b>Order</b></td><td><b>CHART</b> order by default — left to right as drawn, which follows the story — or A–Z by category.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/4fe0e6/4fe0e6.png" alt=""> <b>Installs, works offline</b></td><td>Add it to a home screen or install it from the Info panel where the browser offers that; it opens instantly and works without a connection.</td></tr>
<tr><td width="32%"><img src="https://placehold.co/14x14/8b91a6/8b91a6.png" alt=""> <b>Report an error</b></td><td>Every card has a link that opens a pre-filled issue here. The data is a transcription; corrections are how it gets closer to the game. The Info panel counts how many missions have been checked in-game so far.</td></tr>
</table>

## Keyboard

<table>
<tr><th align="left" width="32%">Key</th><th align="left">Does</th></tr>
<tr><td><code>/</code></td><td>Jump to the search box from anywhere on the page. <code>Enter</code> opens the first result; <code>Esc</code> leaves the box (the query stays) so the map keys work again.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>Tab</code></td><td>Accept the suggested fixer or district filter while typing — it becomes a removable chip under the search.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>[</code> <code>]</code></td><td>Step to the previous or next mission in the list, in whichever order is showing, and centre its tile on the map.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>T</code></td><td>Switch between the TREE and TABLE sheets.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>F</code></td><td>Full screen on / off (there is a button for it at the bottom right).</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>N</code></td><td>Jump to NEXT — the next main mission you haven't done and nothing locks.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>Space</code></td><td>Mark the selected mission done, or undo it. <code>Backspace</code> and <code>Delete</code> unmark.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>0</code></td><td>Fit the whole chart in the window.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>-</code> <code>=</code></td><td>Zoom out and in about the centre, in the same steps as the buttons.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>↑</code> <code>↓</code> <code>←</code> <code>→</code></td><td>Pan the map (<code>W</code> <code>A</code> <code>S</code> <code>D</code> work too). Hold <code>Shift</code> for larger steps. The mouse wheel zooms; <code>Ctrl</code> + wheel zooms faster.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>?</code></td><td>Open the Info panel — how it works, keys, playthroughs.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>Esc</code></td><td>Close whatever is on top: an open panel, then the card, then the search. A click on empty chart does the same.</td></tr>
</table>

## Data and attribution

The chart geometry and mission data were transcribed from a public-domain community flowchart posted to r/cyberpunkgame, rebuilt as vectors. Nothing has been verified against the game itself; treat a mismatch with the game as expected, not as a bug.

The full architecture notes — data shapes, rendering, the swarm, the URL scheme — are in the comment block at the top of `index.html`.
